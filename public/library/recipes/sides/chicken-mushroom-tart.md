## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the volailler, specify boneless, skinless edible weight. Ask for poultry minced fresh if needed; do not replace the same weight with bone-in pieces or cachir.
- Dairy: fromage rouge means gouda/edam-style block cheese for grating, not spreadable triangles.
- Use the dairy fat level listed: crème cuisson around 20% and crème fraîche épaisse around 30% are different. For a milk alternative to 100 ml cream in a sauce, use 100 ml milk mixed cold with 3 g Maïzena, then simmer gently until thick; this changes the figures.
- Produce: champignons may be fresh or plain canned, using the listed weight after draining; dry-fry drained ones briefly before adding sauce.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 170 °C. Use silicone cube molds explicitly rated for baking, supported on a tray; freezer-only plastic molds must not go in the oven. Alternatively bake in a shallow lined dish, then portion the cooked filling into freezer molds.
2. Chop vegetables small. Use the listed fat to soften onion/leek for 8–10 min; add garlic for 1 min. Cook mushrooms until their liquid evaporates, spinach until wilted and drained, and grated carrot, pepper or squash until tender. Cook any raw chicken separately in the same pan to 74 °C. Cool the filling briefly.
3. Beat the eggs with the listed milk, cream or fromage blanc and seasoning; stir in any listed flour and breadcrumbs. Stir in the cooked filling, grated/crumbled cheese and ready-cooked tuna/cachir if listed. Distribute all solid ingredients evenly.
4. Fill oven-safe cavities no more than three-quarters full. Bake about 20–35 min, depending on cavity size, until the centre is set and reaches 74 °C. A shallow dish can take longer. Rest 5 min, then follow the cooling and cube packing steps; keep the cooked filling soft rather than browning it crisp.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **8 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **125 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
