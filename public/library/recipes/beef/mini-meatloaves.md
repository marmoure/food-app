## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the boucherie, ask for the named cut, trimmed and weighed without bone; mince should be hachée devant moi, about 10% fat. Braising cuts need the long cooking method; they are not a direct quick-fry steak substitute.
- Coulis/passata is pourable tomato, not concentrated paste. If cans or coulis are unavailable, use the same weight of ripe peeled grated tomatoes, then simmer uncovered to the stated sauce consistency before molding; add water only if needed.
- Épicerie alternatives: use mild prepared moutarde made without added alcohol, and reduce it to taste if the jar is sharp; check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir; white sugar replaces cassonade gram for gram in this cooked dish.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

Keep the additional tomato and stock sauce ingredients aside while preparing the meat mixture; they are used only in the final sauce step.


1. **Burner 1, pan, medium:** oil, onion and pepper with a pinch of salt for **8 min**, until soft with no raw bite. Garlic for the last minute. Spread on a plate to cool for 10 min.
2. **Oven 200 °C.** In a big bowl, soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 min, then beat in the eggs, mustard, paprika, thyme, pepper and salt.
3. Add the mince, the cooled onion mix and the parsley. Mix with your hands **just until even**; over-mixing makes them dense.
4. Lightly oil **14 silicone muffin cups** set on a tray. Fill each with a scoop (~105 g), pressing it in and smoothing the top.
5. Stir the glaze together and spread a teaspoonful over each.
6. Bake **22–25 min**, until the glaze is sticky and the middle reaches 71 °C (no pink; clear juices). Rest 5 min, then pour off any fat and pop them out.


**Convert to a moist component:** after the meat is fully cooked, remove skewers and cut it into 1–2 cm pieces. In a wide pot simmer the additional **600 g crushed tomatoes + 250 ml water + 1 stock cube** for 10 min. Fold in all the cooked meat and cooking juices and simmer 5 min more, until evenly coated. Fully cook any egg-bound mixture before this step. Divide the meat and sauce evenly into cube molds.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **7 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
