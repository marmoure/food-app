A fully cooked meal component with its starch included, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** vegetable cubes; already contains its starch. **Cooker:** stovetop.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the volailler, specify boneless, skinless edible weight. Ask for poultry minced fresh if needed; do not replace the same weight with bone-in pieces or cachir.
- Dairy: fromage rouge means gouda/edam-style block cheese for grating, not spreadable triangles; choose firm pizza mozzarella; the same weight of grated fromage rouge works with less stretch.
- Use the dairy fat level listed: crème cuisson around 20% and crème fraîche épaisse around 30% are different. For a milk alternative to 100 ml cream in a sauce, use 100 ml milk mixed cold with 3 g Maïzena, then simmer gently until thick; this changes the figures.
- Coulis/passata is pourable tomato, not concentrated paste. If cans or coulis are unavailable, use the same weight of ripe peeled grated tomatoes, then simmer uncovered to the stated sauce consistency before molding; add water only if needed.
- Produce: washed chopped blettes can replace spinach by trimmed weight; cook chopped stalks with the onions before adding the leaves.
- Seasoning alternatives: plain paprika doux replaces sweet smoked paprika in the same amount; use plain dried thyme if oregano is unavailable; zaâtar here means the herb, not a salted sesame blend.
- Épicerie alternatives: check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. **Big pot, medium-high:** oil, then the chicken with ½ tsp salt. Cook **6–7 min**, stirring now and then, until white all over and browned in places. It finishes cooking with the pasta.
2. Medium heat. Add the onion and peppers for **5 min** until softened. Add the garlic, paprikas, thyme, oregano and pepper for **1 min**, then the tomato paste for **1 min**.
3. Add the canned tomatoes and **1 L of stock**, scrape the bottom, and bring to the boil.
4. Stir in the **penne**, bring back to a boil, then turn to a lively simmer, lid off. Cook **10–11 min**, **stirring every 2 min** so it doesn't stick, until the pasta is **just al dente** (1–2 min less than the packet). Add a splash of water if it gets thick before the pasta is ready.
5. Stir in the spinach for **1 min** until wilted.
6. **Heat off.** Stir in the cream, then the mozzarella and fromage rouge until melted. Taste for salt. It should look **saucier than you'd serve it**; the pasta drinks the sauce as it cools and freezes.


**Prepare for cubes:** For molding, cut cooked meat, meatballs, sausage and vegetables to at most 1–2 cm. Stir solids and sauce together before dividing so every serving gets its share. Add only enough hot water to make a dry mixture spoonable; keep cooking juices.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **6**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
