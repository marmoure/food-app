A fully cooked meal component with its starch included, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** vegetable cubes; already contains its starch. **Cooker:** stovetop.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: fromage rouge means gouda/edam-style block cheese for grating, not spreadable triangles; buy unsweetened fromage blanc/jben frais; check the protein label rather than treating a sweet dairy dessert as equivalent.
- Produce: champignons may be fresh or plain canned, using the listed weight after draining; dry-fry drained ones briefly before adding sauce.
- Épicerie alternatives: choose a sauce soja suitable for your halal requirements; if unavailable, replace each 15 ml with 15 ml halal stock plus a small pinch of salt and simmer, accepting a less fermented flavour; check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

**Pressure cooker:** use the manufacturer’s minimum thin-liquid volume and fill limits (at most half full for beans, grains and foaming food). Times start at full pressure. On a stovetop cocotte, sauté with the lid off on the burner; an electric sauté button is not required. Add thick dairy/flour sauces after pressure cooking where possible, then reduce uncovered.


1. **Burner 1, wide heavy pot, medium-high:** 1 tbsp of the oil and **half the mushrooms**, in a single layer, **4 min** without stirring much, until browned. Tip them into a bowl and do the rest the same way. Crowded mushrooms steam instead of browning, and the browning is where the flavour comes from.
2. Turn to medium. The rest of the oil, the onion, carrots and a pinch of salt for **8 min**, until the onion is soft with no raw bite. Add the garlic for 1 min, then the tomato paste for **2 min**, until it darkens and starts to catch on the bottom.
3. Add the mushrooms back, then the **lentils, bay, thyme, paprika, soy sauce and 1.4 L stock**, scraping the bottom. Bring to the boil, then lid on, low simmer for **25–30 min**, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft and most of the liquid is absorbed. Take the lid off for the last 5 min if it's still soupy. It should be thick and spoonable; fish out the bay and taste for salt.
4. **Burner 2, potatoes** (start them when the lentils go in): cover with cold water, 1 tsp salt, boil and simmer **15–20 min** until a knife slides in (or pressure cooker, **8 min**). Drain, steam dry in the hot pot 2 min, then mash with the butter, warm milk and nutmeg using a masher. Beat in the **fromage blanc** last, off the heat. Season.


**Prepare for cubes:** For molding, cut cooked meat, meatballs, sausage and vegetables to at most 1–2 cm. Stir solids and sauce together before dividing so every serving gets its share. Add only enough hot water to make a dry mixture spoonable; keep cooking juices. For assembly, keep the cooked filling and mash separate instead of layering a large pie. Divide each into the same number of shares and freeze each component in its own molds; both sets together make the calculated meal.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **6**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
