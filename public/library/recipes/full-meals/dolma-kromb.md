## Ingredients (6 recipe servings)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|

| Beef mince, lean, raw (viande hachée maigre) | 850 g |
| Rice, long-grain, dry (riz) | 200 g |
| Onion, grated into the filling | 150 g |
| Garlic (thoum) | 10 g |
| Parsley (maadnous) | 30 g |
| Pepper, cinnamon, nutmeg, coriander | 8 g |
| Vegetable oil (huile de table) | 35 g |
| Onion, for the sauce | 150 g |
| Chickpeas, cooked or canned, drained | 400 g |
| Stock cube (bouillon) | 10 g |
| Lemon juice (from lemons) | 15 g |
| Salt | 10 g |

Water used for poaching or loosening is specified in the method. Weigh drained canned foods and edible meat; season lightly and taste after cooking.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the boucherie, ask for the named cut, trimmed and weighed without bone; mince should be hachée devant moi, about 10% fat. Braising cuts need the long cooking method; they are not a direct quick-fry steak substitute.
- Legume weights in this dish are cooked and drained. Cook dried hommos or loubia separately and weigh the required cooked amount; do not use the listed cooked weight as a dry weight. White beans can replace red beans in the same cooked weight.
- Épicerie alternatives: check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Cook the rice separately until just tender. Work on the filling while it cooks, then combine promptly; get the finished shallow portions into refrigeration or freezing within 1 hour of the rice finishing.
2. Finely chop all onion and garlic. Dice cabbage, peppers or zucchini to 1–2 cm as listed; there is no stuffing or rolling.
3. In the oil, soften all onion for 8 min. Add garlic and beef mince, breaking it up, and cook until the mince reaches 71 °C. Add seasoning and any listed tomato/paste for 2 min.
4. Add the diced vegetables, stock cube dissolved in 400 ml water, and cooked drained chickpeas. Cover and simmer 15–25 min until vegetables are tender, adding water if needed. Reduce uncovered to a thick, moist stew.
5. Fold in cooked rice, herbs and lemon if listed. Heat through and divide evenly into molds. The mixture contains both protein and starch; no egg binder is needed.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **6 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. For this rice dish, cool and refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **1 hour** of cooking. For other ingredients, refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
