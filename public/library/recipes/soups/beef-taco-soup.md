A cooked soup component, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** rice or mash cubes; add a chicken cube if more protein is needed. **Cooker:** stovetop + pressure cooker.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the boucherie, ask for the named cut, trimmed and weighed without bone; mince should be hachée devant moi, about 10% fat. Braising cuts need the long cooking method; they are not a direct quick-fry steak substitute.
- Buy dried legumes at the épicerie; soak overnight in the fridge in hot weather, discard that water and cook until fully tender. Keep legumes and liquid below the pressure cooker half-full mark. Older beans may need longer; acidic tomato sauce is added after they soften.
- Seasoning alternatives: if powders are unavailable, replace 1 tsp garlic powder with 10 g finely crushed garlic and 1 tsp onion powder with 40 g finely grated onion, fully softened and dried in the recipe’s cooking fat before mixing in; parsley replaces dill by the same fresh or dried quantity; use plain dried thyme if oregano is unavailable; zaâtar here means the herb, not a salted sesame blend.
- Épicerie alternatives: check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

**Before the dish:** soak red kidney beans at least 5 hours (overnight in the fridge is practical), discard the soaking water, and boil briskly in fresh water for at least 30 min. Continue until fully tender and drain. Use these already-cooked beans in the recipe; skip any later pressure step whose sole purpose is cooking beans, and add the beans after other meat is tender. Never put merely soaked kidney beans in a slow cooker. [FDA bean guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/chemical-contaminants-pesticides/natural-toxins-food).


**Pressure cooker:** use the manufacturer’s minimum thin-liquid volume and fill limits (at most half full for beans, grains and foaming food). Times start at full pressure. On a stovetop cocotte, sauté with the lid off on the burner; an electric sauté button is not required. Add thick dairy/flour sauces after pressure cooking where possible, then reduce uncovered.


1. Cook and drain all listed dried legumes separately until tender, using the kidney-bean preparation above for red beans; other legumes can follow their pressure-cooker or packet method.
2. **8 L pot on medium-high,** meanwhile: oil and mince for **8 min**, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned with no pink left. Spoon off any pool of fat.
3. Add the onions and peppers for **6 min**, until soft.
4. Turn to low. Add the garlic and all the dried spices and herbs for **1 min**, stirring, then the tomato paste for **2 min**.
5. Add the tomatoes, beans, corn, stock cubes and **2 L water**. Bring to the boil, then lid ajar and simmer **20 min**, stirring now and then.
6. Taste for salt. It should be thick and chunky with enough broth to ladle.


**Prepare for cubes:** For molding, cut cooked meat, meatballs, sausage and vegetables to at most 1–2 cm. Stir solids and sauce together before dividing so every serving gets its share. Add only enough hot water to make a dry mixture spoonable; keep cooking juices.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **10**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
