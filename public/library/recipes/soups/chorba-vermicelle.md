A cooked soup component, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** rice or mash cubes; add a chicken cube if more protein is needed. **Cooker:** stovetop.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the boucherie, ask for the named cut, trimmed and weighed without bone; mince should be hachée devant moi, about 10% fat. Braising cuts need the long cooking method; they are not a direct quick-fry steak substitute.
- Produce: use local krafs stems by weight; with a very leafy bunch use a few leaves for flavour and make up the missing stem weight with carrot.
- Legume weights in this dish are cooked and drained. Cook dried hommos or loubia separately and weigh the required cooked amount; do not use the listed cooked weight as a dry weight. White beans can replace red beans in the same cooked weight.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. **Kefta first:** mix the mince with the chopped parsley, ½ tsp salt, a pinch of pepper and a pinch of cinnamon. Roll about **40 marbles** (10 g each, a heaped teaspoon). Keep them covered in the fridge.
2. **8 L pot on medium:** oil and smen, then the beef cubes and grated onion. Cook **8–10 min**, stirring, until the meat has lost its red colour and the onion is soft.
3. Add the paprika, black pepper, half the coriander and the fresh mint for **1 min**, then the blended tomatoes and the cinnamon stick. Cook **3 min**.
4. Add the carrots, potatoes, celery, 2 tsp salt and **2.3 L boiling water**. Lid ajar, simmer **50–60 min**, until a beef cube breaks when you press it with a spoon.
5. Dilute the tomato paste in a ladle of the broth and stir it in. Add the zucchini, chickpeas and kefta (drop them in one by one so they hold together). Simmer **10 min**; cut one meatball open: no pink inside.
6. Shower in the vermicelli, stirring so it doesn't clump, and simmer **3–4 min only**. It should still have a little bite.
7. **Off the heat:** the rest of the coriander and the dried mint. Fish out the cinnamon stick and taste for salt.
8. Leave it brothy, a little thinner than you'd serve it: the vermicelli keeps drinking the liquid. You want about 5.2 L; top up with boiling water if you're short.


**Prepare for cubes:** For molding, cut cooked meat, meatballs, sausage and vegetables to at most 1–2 cm. Stir solids and sauce together before dividing so every serving gets its share. Add only enough hot water to make a dry mixture spoonable; keep cooking juices.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **10**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
