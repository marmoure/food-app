A soft cooked vegetable component, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** protein cubes + rice, pasta, couscous or mash cubes. **Cooker:** oven.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Ingredients (24 recipe servings; cube yield measured after cooking)
| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| Cauliflower (chou-fleur) | 1 large head (~1.5 kg of florets) | small florets |
| Potatoes (batata) | ~1.2 kg as bought (1 kg peeled) | 8 mm slices |
| White vinegar (khal) | 1 tbsp | for the cauliflower water |
| **Béchamel:** butter (beurre) | 40 g | plus a little for the dish |
| Flour (farine) | 50 g | |
| Milk (lait demi-écrémé) | 800 ml | |
| Nutmeg, salt, black pepper | a pinch, 1½ tsp, ½ tsp | |
| Eggs | 3 | |
| Parsley (maadnous) | small bunch (~20 g) | chopped |
| Gouda / edam (fromage rouge), or emmental | 120 g | grated |

**Spinach variant:** soften ½ onion in 1 tbsp oil for 8 min, then stir it into the béchamel with 400 g cooked spinach (*sebnekh*, fresh or frozen) squeezed very dry. It adds about 5 kcal a square.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: fromage rouge means gouda/edam-style block cheese for grating, not spreadable triangles.

## Method

1. **Béchamel first, so it can cool:** melt the butter in a pot over medium heat, stir in the flour for **1 min**, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer **5 min**, whisking, until thick. Season with nutmeg, salt and pepper and leave it to cool for 15 min.
2. **Cauliflower:** boil it in salted water with the vinegar for **5 min**, until just tender. Drain it in a colander for **10 min** (wet cauliflower is what makes a gratin watery).
3. **Potatoes:** boil the slices in salted water for **8 min**, until just tender, and drain.
4. **Oven 180 °C.** Butter a large roasting dish (about 30 × 40 cm, or two smaller ones). Lay the potato slices over the bottom, then the cauliflower on top.
5. Whisk the eggs and parsley into the cooled béchamel and pour it evenly over the veg. Scatter the cheese over.
6. Bake **25–30 min**, until the middle is set (a knife comes out with no runny sauce) and the top is golden. Finish with **3–4 min** under the grill for colour.


**Prepare for cubes:** Chop cooked vegetables to 1–2 cm and press gently into the mold. For a loose roasted mixture, mash a spoonful of the vegetables into each portion with a little of its cooking juice to bind it. Reheated cubes will be soft; crisp edges are not the target.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **24**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **125 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
