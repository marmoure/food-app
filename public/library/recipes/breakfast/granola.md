## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: yaourt nature must be unsweetened; brassé describes texture and does not guarantee Greek-yogurt protein.
- Épicerie alternatives: white sugar replaces cassonade gram for gram in this cooked dish.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160 °C. Mix oats, nuts, seeds, coconut, seasoning, pumpkin purée, honey, oil and sugar. Spread thinly on a tray and bake 25–35 min, stirring twice, until the oats are cooked and lightly golden.
2. Cool the oat mixture promptly on a shallow tray. Mix in raisins, then stir all of it into the plain unsweetened yogurt.
3. Divide yogurt and oats evenly into cube molds and freeze promptly. This is a cold breakfast component; the oats soften in yogurt rather than staying crisp.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**No reheat:** eat directly from the freezer as a cold component, allowing 5–10 min to soften if needed. Combine with compatible cold breakfast or dessert cubes, serve immediately and do not refreeze the softened portion.
