## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: buy unsweetened fromage blanc/jben frais; check the protein label rather than treating a sweet dairy dessert as equivalent.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Blend the fromage blanc, eggs, milk and vanilla sugar until smooth. Grind the oats to a coarse flour, then stir in the flour, milk powder, sugar, baking powder and salt. Mix with the wet ingredients and the oil; rest 10 min.
2. Fill oven-rated silicone cube molds at most three-quarters full, or spread in a lined shallow dish. Bake 25–40 min until set and at least 74 °C in the centre. There is no pan-frying at meal time.
3. Cool briefly, portion into cube molds if needed and freeze. Reheat as a soft breakfast component alongside compatible fruit or yogurt cubes.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
