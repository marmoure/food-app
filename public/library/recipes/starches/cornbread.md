## Ingredients (12 recipe servings)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cornmeal (farine de maïs) | 250 g |
| Flour, white (farine) | 250 g |
| Sugar (sucre) | 50 g |
| Baking powder and soda | 16 g |
| Salt | 6 g |
| Lben (fermented milk) | 450 g |
| Eggs (œufs), ~50 g each without shell | 100 g without shell (about 2 eggs) |
| Butter (beurre) | 80 g |
| Honey (asal / miel) | 40 g |
| Vegetable oil (huile de table) | 20 g |

Water used for poaching or loosening is specified in the method. Weigh drained canned foods and edible meat; season lightly and taste after cooking.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- The listed ingredients are ordinary souk/épicerie items. Buy by metric weight; packet sizes and the size of individual vegetables vary.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

- Farine/semoule de maïs means ground maize, not Maïzena starch. If unavailable, fine wheat semolina can make a different soft semolina version; use the same weight and adjust moisture to the stated consistency.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Mix cornmeal (not Maïzena), flour, sugar, raising agents and salt. Whisk lben, eggs, honey and melted cooled butter separately, then fold together without overmixing.
2. Use the oil to grease a lined shallow baking dish or oven-rated silicone cube molds. Fill cavities only two-thirds full. Bake 20–35 min until set and a skewer shows no wet batter; check 74 °C in the middle.
3. Portion the soft baked bread into cube molds if needed, keeping each nutritional serving together. Reheat as a moist starch component with soup or a saucy protein; the sausage variant is omitted.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **12 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **125 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
