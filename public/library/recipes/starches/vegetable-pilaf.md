A cooked starch component, adapted for cube molds and shopping in Oum El Bouaghi. Cook the complete recipe first, then freeze in molds and assemble with other frozen components.

**Assembly:** a saucy protein cube + vegetable cubes. **Cooker:** stovetop.

The stated number of servings divides the batch for nutrition. It does not predict the number of mold cavities; weigh and label each serving when packing.

## Ingredients (10 recipe servings; cube yield measured after cooking)
| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| Long-grain rice (riz), or basmati if it's a fair price | 600 g | rinsed until the water runs clear, drained 10 min |
| Olive oil (zit zitoun) | 2½ tbsp (30 g) | |
| Onion (basal) | 1 (~150 g) | finely chopped |
| Carrots (zroudiya) | 2 (~200 g) | finely diced |
| Celery (krafes) | 2 sticks (~100 g) | finely diced |
| Zucchini (qar'a / courgette) | 1 (~200 g) | finely diced |
| Garlic (thoum) | 2 cloves (10 g) | crushed |
| Turmeric (kourkoum) | ½ tsp | |
| Black pepper (felfel k'hal), salt | ½ tsp, 1 tsp | |
| Chicken stock cubes | 2 | dissolved in 1.25 L hot water |
| Fresh dill (aneth) and parsley (maadnous) | a small handful each (~15 g each) | chopped (or 1 tsp dried dill) |
| Lemon juice (citron) | 1 tbsp (~20 ml) | |

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Produce: use local krafs stems by weight; with a very leafy bunch use a few leaves for flavour and make up the missing stem weight with carrot.
- Seasoning alternatives: parsley replaces dill by the same fresh or dried quantity.
- Épicerie alternatives: check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. **Widest heavy pot with a lid, medium-high:** oil, onion, carrot and celery for **5 min**, until just tender. Add the zucchini, garlic, turmeric, pepper and salt for **1 min**.
2. Add the drained rice and stir for **2 min**, until every grain is coated and smells toasty.
3. Pour in the **1.25 L hot stock**, bring to a rolling boil, then put the lid on and turn the heat to the lowest setting for **15 min**. Don't lift the lid.
4. Check: the top should have little steam holes and no liquid should pool when you push the rice aside with a fork. If there's liquid, give it 2–3 min more. Off the heat, lid on, rest **10 min**.
5. Fork it through gently, folding in the dill, parsley and lemon juice. **Spread it on 2 trays straight away** so it stops cooking and steams off for 5–10 min.
6. While it's still warm, press it firmly into the silicone muffin trays, about 105 g per well, 20 wells.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the cooked batch (without the pot) and divide by **10**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute meat, sauce and vegetables evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of cooking, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. For this rice dish, aim to get the shallow portions into the fridge or freezer within **1 hour**. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl. Add 15–30 ml water to rice, pasta or thick sauce, and cover loosely. At **800 W**, start with **3 min**, break up and stir, then heat in **1 min bursts**, stirring between each, until the centre of every component reaches **74 °C**. Rest **1 min** and check again. A mixed bowl or several cubes takes longer; use temperature, not a fixed total time. Add fragile cream sauces in shorter bursts once loosened. No prior defrosting or fresh garnish is required. Reheat only what you will eat; do not refreeze reheated food.
