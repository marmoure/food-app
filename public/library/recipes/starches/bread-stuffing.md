## Ingredients (10 recipe servings)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Baguette | 600 g |
| Butter (beurre) | 60 g |
| Onions (basal) | 300 g |
| Celery (krafes / céleri) | 200 g |
| Sage, thyme, pepper | 4 g |
| Parsley (maadnous) | 20 g |
| Stock cube (bouillon) | 20 g |
| Eggs (œufs), ~50 g each without shell | 100 g without shell (about 2 eggs) |
| Salt | 3 g |

Water used for poaching or loosening is specified in the method. Weigh drained canned foods and edible meat; season lightly and taste after cooking.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Produce: use local krafs stems by weight; with a very leafy bunch use a few leaves for flavour and make up the missing stem weight with carrot.
- Épicerie alternatives: check bouillon and processed meats are halal; mild fresh chicken sausage is different from cooked cachir.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Dice the bread into 1 cm pieces. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Soften chopped onion and celery in the butter for 10 min; add herbs.
2. Dissolve the stock cubes in 450 ml hot water and let cool to warm. Beat the eggs into the warm stock, then mix with bread, softened vegetables, parsley and seasoning. Let the bread absorb the liquid for 10 min; add a little water if any pieces remain dry.
3. Press into a lined shallow baking dish and bake covered for 25 min, then uncover for about 10 min until set and 74 °C in the centre. This version is soft and moist, not a crisp bread side. Press cooked portions into cube molds and cool promptly.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **10 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **125 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
