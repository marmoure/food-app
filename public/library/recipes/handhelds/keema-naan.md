## Local supplies and substitutions

- At the boucherie, ask for the named cut, trimmed and weighed without bone; mince should be hachée devant moi, about 10% fat. Braising cuts need the long cooking method; they are not a direct quick-fry steak substitute.
- Seasoning alternatives: for 1 tsp garam masala, mix ½ tsp ground coriander, ¼ tsp cumin, ⅛ tsp cinnamon and ⅛ tsp black pepper; optional cardamom/clove is only a pinch.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Dice onion, garlic and other vegetables finely; cut chicken to 1–2 cm or break mince into small pieces. Drain canned ingredients and grate the cheese. Mix the listed dried seasonings, using only mild spices.
2. Heat the listed cooking fat in a wide pot. Cook onion and firm vegetables for 8–10 min until soft; cook garlic and spices for 1 min. Add the meat and cook, stirring, until fully cooked: poultry 74 °C, beef mince 71 °C.
3. Add the listed tomato/paste, cooked beans, sweetcorn, condiments and other filling ingredients; keep cheese and finishing herbs/lemon aside. Add 150–250 ml water as needed and simmer 10–15 min, until vegetables are tender and the mixture is moist and spoonable, not dry crumbles. Fold spinach in for the last 5 min.
4. Stir in the listed cheese, herbs and lemon juice, if used. Melt the cheese gently into the hot filling. Remove whole spice fragments and divide solids and sauce evenly into molds.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **6 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **250 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
