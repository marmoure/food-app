## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: fromage rouge means gouda/edam-style block cheese for grating, not spreadable triangles; if feta is unavailable, use the same weight of firm, well-drained plain jben, crumble it and season to taste; do not use dried klila as a direct substitute.
- For each **50 g drained sun-dried tomatoes**, a local alternative is **100 g sweet red pepper + 15 g tomato concentrate**: dice and cook the pepper with the onion until soft, then fry the paste for 1 min. Reduce the extra moisture before freezing.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. **Oven to 180 °C.** Oil three loaf tins (~25 cm) and line the bases with baking paper.
2. **Veg**, one burner, medium-high, in a large frying pan: 1 tsp oil and the courgettes with a pinch of salt for **6–8 min**, until tender and all their water has cooked off (wet courgettes make a soggy cake). Add the garlic for the last minute. Tip into a bowl to cool. In the same pan, cook the diced peppers for **8–10 min** until soft and catching at the edges. Tip into a second bowl.
3. **Batter:** whisk the eggs, milk and olive oil in a large bowl. Mix the flour, baking powder, pepper and pinch of salt separately, then whisk them into the eggs in three goes, just until smooth (a few lumps are fine). Stir in the grated gouda.
4. **Divide the batter** between three bowls, about **435 g each**.
5. Fold the fillings in gently, one per bowl: **A** courgettes, feta and mint; **B** peppers, olives and camembert; **C** sun-dried tomatoes, basil and feta. (Tossing the diced cheese and veg in a spoon of flour first stops them sinking.)
6. Pour into the tins; scatter the sunflower seeds over loaf C. Mark each tin (a strip of paper under the edge) so you know which is which later.
7. **Bake all three together 40–45 min**, until risen, golden and a knife in the middle comes out clean.
8. Cool in the tins **10 min**, turn out onto a rack and cool **30 min** more. Cut each loaf into **10 slices** (~2.5 cm).


**Cube version:** use the completed baked mixture, including any listed glaze, after it has set. Cut into pieces smaller than a mold cavity and press gently into 60 ml freezer molds; use the listed moist fruit/filling to bind crumbs, not raw batter. Pack separate flavours separately. This savoury cube is a side component; combine it with a protein and vegetable cube.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **60 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
