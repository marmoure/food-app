## Method

1. **Oven to 180 °C.** Line a 20 × 30 cm baking tray with baking paper, leaving some overhang on the long sides so you can lift the slab out.
2. **Brown the butter**, one burner, medium heat, in a light-coloured saucepan: melt it and keep stirring for **5–7 min**. It foams, then the solids sink and turn deep golden-brown and it smells nutty. Take it off the heat straight away (it goes from brown to burnt in seconds).
3. Put both sugars in a large bowl, pour the hot butter over and **whisk 1 min** until smooth. Leave it 5 min so it's warm, not hot.
4. Whisk in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla sugar, until glossy.
5. In another bowl, mix the flour, baking powder and salt. Fold them into the wet mix with a spatula until no dry flour shows. Don't beat it; over-mixing makes them cakey.
6. Fold in most of the white chocolate and peanuts, spread the batter evenly in the tray, and scatter the rest on top.
7. **Bake 25–30 min**, until the edges are golden and a knife in the centre comes out with a few moist crumbs but no wet batter. They firm up as they cool.
8. Cool in the tray for **15 min**, lift the slab out on its paper and cool on a rack for another 30 min. Cut into **15 bars** (3 × 5).


**Cube version:** use the completed baked mixture, including any listed glaze, after it has set. Cut into pieces smaller than a mold cavity and press gently into 60 ml freezer molds; use the listed moist fruit/filling to bind crumbs, not raw batter. Pack separate flavours separately. A cake cube is a dessert component; serve it warm with a separately frozen fruit or yogurt component.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **60 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**Microwave from frozen:** place the labeled serving of cubes in a microwave-safe bowl and cover loosely. Add 15–30 ml water if the mixture is thick or dry. At 800 W start with 1 min for a small dessert/side cube, or 3 min for a meal serving, then break up and stir. Continue in 30–60 s bursts until the centre reaches **74 °C**. Rest 1 min and check again. Several cubes take longer; temperature determines readiness. Assemble with compatible already frozen cubes. The component is ready to assemble directly from frozen. Do not refreeze reheated food.
