## Local supplies and substitutions

- Épicerie alternatives: unsweetened peanut butter can be made by processing roasted unsalted cacahuètes until smooth; weigh the paste needed.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

- If chia or ground flaxseed is unavailable, use the same weight of finely ground ready-to-eat oats in these bound dessert mixtures; add water only a teaspoon at a time if needed. This changes the fibre/fat contribution.

## Method

1. **A, apple:** grated apple in a bowl, covered, **microwave 4–5 min**, stir, then 1–2 min more uncovered until soft and most of the juice has gone. Cool 10 min.
2. **A, food processor:** oats, raisins and cinnamon until the oats are fine. Add the cooked apple, peanut butter, honey, chia and salt and pulse until it clumps. If it's too wet to roll, add a spoon of oats. Tip into a bowl and chill **30 min** in the fridge.
3. **B, food processor** (no need to wash it): peanuts and pistachios, pulsed into coarse crumbs. Add the dates and raisins and process until sticky, then the oats, honey, chia and salt, and pulse until it holds when squeezed. Chill **30 min**.
4. Mix the flaxseed into batch A. Press both completed mixtures into 60 ml molds, keeping the two flavours separate.


**Cube version:** instead of forming balls or cutting bars, press the completed mixtures into 60 ml molds, including their chocolate or nut layers. Keep any different flavours separate. Never add uncooked flour; the oat and nut ingredients here are ready to eat.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **60 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**No reheat:** eat directly from the freezer as a cold component, allowing 5–10 min to soften if needed. Combine with compatible cold breakfast or dessert cubes, serve immediately and do not refreeze the softened portion.
