## Ingredients (15 recipe servings)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plain biscuits (petits beurre / Lotus type) | 225 g |
| Butter (beurre) | 45 g |
| Fromage blanc / jben frais | 300 g |
| Plain yogurt (yaourt nature) | 500 g |
| Sugar (sucre) | 50 g |
| Peanut butter (beurre de cacahuète) | 120 g |
| Crème fraîche épaisse ~30% | 100 ml |
| Vanilla sugar (sucre vanillé) | 8 g |
| Salt | 1 g |

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: buy unsweetened fromage blanc/jben frais; check the protein label rather than treating a sweet dairy dessert as equivalent; yaourt nature must be unsweetened; brassé describes texture and does not guarantee Greek-yogurt protein.
- Use the dairy fat level listed: crème cuisson around 20% and crème fraîche épaisse around 30% are different. For a milk alternative to 100 ml cream in a sauce, use 100 ml milk mixed cold with 3 g Maïzena, then simmer gently until thick; this changes the figures.
- Épicerie alternatives: unsweetened peanut butter can be made by processing roasted unsalted cacahuètes until smooth; weigh the paste needed.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Crush all the plain petit beurre biscuits finely. Melt the butter and mix with the biscuits.
2. Whisk unsweetened fromage blanc, plain yogurt, icing sugar, vanilla sugar and peanut butter smooth. Whip the cold 30% cream to soft peaks, then fold it in along with the biscuit mixture and pinch of salt.
3. Spoon the complete dessert mixture into 60 ml molds and freeze promptly. It is a frozen dessert cube, not a baked cake; use plain biscuits and homemade peanut butter rather than depending on Lotus products.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **60 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**No reheat:** eat directly from the freezer as a cold component, allowing 5–10 min to soften if needed. Combine with compatible cold breakfast or dessert cubes, serve immediately and do not refreeze the softened portion.
