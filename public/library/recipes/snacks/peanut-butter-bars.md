## Ingredients (15 recipe servings)

| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| Petit beurre biscuits (petits beurre) | 100 g (~12 biscuits) | blitzed to fine crumbs |
| Butter (beurre) | 70 g | |
| Peanut butter, smooth (beurre de cacahuète) | 290 g | 260 g for the base, 30 g for the top |
| Icing sugar (sucre glace) | 120 g | |
| Dark cooking chocolate (chocolat noir pâtissier) | 200 g | broken into squares |
| Roasted salted peanuts (cacahuètes grillées salées) | 50 g | roughly chopped |

No peanut butter in the shop? Blitz 300 g roasted peanuts with a pinch of salt in the food processor for 5–8 min, scraping down, until runny. It's cheaper than the jar.

## Method

1. **Line a 20 × 20 cm dish** (or a 20 × 25 cm one) with baking paper, leaving an overhang so you can lift the slab out.
2. **Biscuit crumbs:** blitz the biscuits in the food processor until fine and even, like sand.
3. **Base:** put the butter and 260 g peanut butter in a microwave-safe bowl. **Microwave 30 s**, stir, then 15 s more if needed, just until they melt together. Don't get it piping hot.
4. Stir in the icing sugar and biscuit crumbs to a thick, even paste. Press it firmly and evenly into the dish with the back of a spoon.
5. **Top:** chocolate and the last 30 g peanut butter in a clean bowl. **Microwave 30 s**, stir, then **15 s bursts**, stirring each time, until just melted and smooth. Stop while a few lumps remain; stirring melts them.
6. Pour over the base, spread it smooth, and scatter the chopped peanuts over, pressing them in lightly.
7. **Fridge for 2 h** until completely set. Lift the slab out and cut into **15 bars** (3 × 5) with a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between cuts.


**Cube version:** instead of forming balls or cutting bars, press the completed mixtures into 60 ml molds, including their chocolate or nut layers. Keep any different flavours separate. Never add uncooked flour; the oat and nut ingredients here are ready to eat.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the finished batch and divide into **15 equal recipe servings**, distributing all ingredients evenly. Use **60 ml food-safe cube molds**. One serving can occupy several cavities; bag the complete set together and label its cube count. Leave the mold’s expansion space.
2. Refrigerate or freeze shallow portions within **2 hours** of cooking/mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**. Freeze in one layer at **−18 °C**; use the freezer’s actual rated capacity. If it is full, refrigerate promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity allows.
3. Once solid, unmold into labeled zip bags; vacuum-seal only frozen cubes. Use within one month. See [cube packing](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**No reheat:** eat directly from the freezer as a cold component, allowing 5–10 min to soften if needed. Combine with compatible cold breakfast or dessert cubes, serve immediately and do not refreeze the softened portion.
