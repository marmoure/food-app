Yogurt, fruit and nuts frozen together as dessert cubes. This cold dessert component is eaten directly from the freezer, separate from hot meal cubes.

## Ingredients (15 recipe servings; cube yield measured after cooking)
| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| Plain unsweetened yogurt (yaourt nature) | 2 kg | |
| Honey (asal / miel) | 120 g (6 tbsp) | |
| Vanilla sugar (sucre vanillé) | 2 sachets (16 g) | |
| Fruit in season | 500 g | strawberries in spring, pomegranate seeds or figs in autumn, or frozen red fruit (fruits rouges surgelés) straight from the bag |
| Peanut butter (beurre de cacahuète) | 120 g | |
| Roasted peanuts, unsalted (cacahuètes grillées) | 60 g | chopped |
| Dark chocolate (chocolat noir) | 60 g | |

The base uses ordinary unstrained plain yogurt. Straining changes both the yield and the nutritional concentration, so it is not assumed in the calculation.

## Local supplies and substitutions

- Dairy: yaourt nature must be unsweetened; brassé describes texture and does not guarantee Greek-yogurt protein.
- Épicerie alternatives: unsweetened peanut butter can be made by processing roasted unsalted cacahuètes until smooth; weigh the paste needed.
- The calculation covers the main ingredient list. Alternatives change nutrition and cost; figures are ingredient estimates, not a guarantee for every local brand. There is no grocery budget cap.

## Method

1. Chop fruit into small pieces. Use washed fresh ripe fruit; if a frozen-fruit package says to cook it, follow those instructions and chill the fruit promptly before mixing with yogurt.
2. Stir the plain yogurt, honey and vanilla sugar together. Warm the peanut butter briefly so it stirs easily, then fold it in with the fruit and chopped peanuts.
3. Melt the chocolate in 20 s microwave bursts, stirring between them. Ripple it through the yogurt mixture so every portion contains some.
4. Divide evenly into **125 ml cube molds** and freeze promptly. Keep these as cold dessert cubes rather than a hot meal ingredient.

## Freeze

1. Weigh the mixed batch (without the pot) and divide by **15**. That weight is one recipe serving. Distribute yogurt, fruit and nuts evenly. Use **125 ml food-safe cube molds**, leaving the maker's expansion space. A serving may occupy more than one cavity; bag all its cubes together, including any smaller final cube. Do not assume grams equal millilitres.
2. Portion promptly into shallow molds. Refrigerate or freeze within **2 hours** of mixing, or **1 hour above 32 °C**; do not wait for a whole pot to cool. Freeze in a single layer at **−18 °C** until solid. If the freezer has no capacity, chill promptly at **4 °C or below** and freeze as soon as capacity is available.
3. Unmold and bag by component or serving. Label the dish, date and **number of cubes per recipe serving**. Vacuum-seal only once solid. Use within one month for this system. See [the cube packing guide](../../docs/packing.md).

## Reheat

**No reheat:** eat the dessert cubes straight from the freezer with other frozen dessert components, or let them soften for 5–10 min just before eating. Serve immediately; do not mix them into a hot bowl or refreeze the softened portion.
